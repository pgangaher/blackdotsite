Overview

Identity-first security,
built for zero trust.

Compromised credentials are the root cause of over 80% of breaches. As an RSA consulting partner, we help enterprises replace password-dependent access with modern, phishing-resistant authentication — deploying RSA ID Plus and RSA SecurID across your entire user, device and application estate.

Whether you are modernising a legacy on-prem directory or securing a complex hybrid cloud environment, our certified consultants design and implement identity architectures that enforce zero trust at every access point — without disrupting your workforce.

What We Deliver

  • ✓ RSA ID Plus platform deployment across cloud, hybrid, and on-prem environments
  • ✓ Passwordless authentication rollout using FIDO2, biometrics, and hardware tokens
  • ✓ Identity governance & lifecycle management configuration and policy enforcement
  • ✓ Ongoing MFA policy management, user onboarding, and compliance reporting support

Technologies & Capabilities

A unified identity security platform.

End-to-end identity protection — from passwordless login to governance, compliance, and non-human identity management.

Passwordless Authentication

Replace passwords with FIDO2-compliant phishing-resistant credentials — biometrics, hardware tokens, and mobile push — eliminating the most common attack vector at source.

Adaptive Multi-Factor Authentication

Context-aware MFA that evaluates risk signals — user behaviour, device posture, location — and steps up authentication when anomalies are detected, without burdening low-risk users.

Single Sign-On (SSO)

One secure credential granting seamless access across all cloud, SaaS, and on-prem applications — reducing password fatigue and improving productivity while maintaining strict access controls.

Identity Governance & Lifecycle

Policy-driven access management across user, device, and non-human identities — automating provisioning, de-provisioning, access reviews, and certifications to enforce least-privilege at scale.

Identity Security Posture Management

Continuous, real-time visibility into identity risk across your entire estate — detecting over-privileged accounts, dormant credentials, and misconfigurations before they become incidents.

Directory & Workforce Access

Centralised workforce access management bridging legacy on-prem Active Directory with modern cloud identity providers — maintaining consistent policy across hybrid environments.

Non-Human Identity Management

Extend identity governance beyond users to machine identities, service accounts, and API credentials — ensuring every automated process operates with the least-privilege access it needs.

Hybrid & Cloud-Native Support

Consistent identity enforcement across AWS, Azure, GCP, and on-prem infrastructure — with enhanced integration for Microsoft Entra ID and leading cloud identity platforms.

Compliance & Audit Reporting

Automated governance reports and audit trails for SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, and India's DPDP Act — reducing compliance burden while demonstrating robust identity controls to auditors.
