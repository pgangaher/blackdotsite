Protect, detect and recover. A zero trust data security architecture for hybrid environments — with immutable backups, AI-driven threat detection and orchestrated recovery. Powered by our partnership with Rubrik.

Overview

Your last line
of defence.

When prevention fails, recovery determines survival. Ransomware attacks don't just encrypt files — they target backups, exfiltrate data, and hold entire organisations hostage. Traditional backup solutions were never designed for this threat landscape.

As a Rubrik consulting partner, we implement Rubrik Security Cloud to ensure your data is immutable, your sensitive information is classified and governed, and your recovery is orchestrated and guaranteed — regardless of whether your workloads live on-premises, in the cloud, or in SaaS applications.

What We Deliver

  • ✓ Rubrik Security Cloud deployment across hybrid and multi-cloud environments
  • ✓ Ransomware recovery planning with immutable backup architecture
  • ✓ Sensitive data discovery, classification, and compliance governance
  • ✓ Cyber recovery runbooks and orchestrated restore testing

Key Technology Pillars

A zero trust foundation for your data.

Centralised, cloud-delivered protection across cloud, SaaS and on-premises systems.

Unified Data Security Platform

A centralised, cloud-delivered platform providing visibility and protection for data across hybrid environments — cloud applications, SaaS platforms and on-premises systems.

Zero Trust Security Model

A "never trust, always verify" approach where every user and action is authenticated and authorised — preventing unauthorised access at every layer.

Immutable Backups & Ransomware Protection

Critical data is protected with backups that cannot be modified or deleted — ensuring guaranteed recovery even when systems are compromised by ransomware.

AI-Driven Threat Detection & Recovery

Artificial intelligence continuously monitors for risks, identifies potential threats and enables faster, automated recovery of data after an incident.

Data Visibility & Threat Analytics

Identifies sensitive data such as personal or financial information and monitors unusual behaviour — like sudden encryption or access spikes — to detect breaches early.

Business Continuity & Rapid Recovery

Quickly restore applications, files and databases — minimising downtime and maintaining business operations during disruptions.
