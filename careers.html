Why Black Dot

Six reasons to make the move.

We're not a ticket-grinding firm. We're a small team of specialists who go deep — and we want people who thrive doing the same.

Real enterprise security work

Every project you touch protects live infrastructure at banks, fintechs, and high-growth tech companies. No sandbox projects, no fake data — your work ships into production and matters on day one.

Hands-on with best-in-class tools

Get certified and operate Snyk, Datadog, Palo Alto Networks, Rubrik, RSA, and more. We invest in training because our competitive advantage is your expertise — and that alignment is built into how we run every engagement.

Sponsored certifications & learning

Black Dot funds your professional growth. From CISSP and CISM to vendor-specific certifications across our partner ecosystem — if it makes you better at your craft, we cover the cost and give you the time to prepare.

Fast-track career growth

We're growing fast and building for scale. Joining now means you won't spend years waiting for a title change — strong contributors move up quickly, take on lead roles, and directly influence how the firm operates and grows.

Senior mentorship from day one

Our leadership team brings 20+ combined years in enterprise security consulting. You'll work directly alongside principals — not buried in a hierarchy — with structured 1-on-1 mentorship built into how we onboard and develop everyone.

Flexibility & work-life respect

We operate on outcomes, not optics. Flexible working arrangements, reasonable hours, and a culture that doesn't confuse presence with performance. Security is a high-stakes field — your energy and wellbeing are assets we protect too.

Our Process

Straightforward, respectful, fast.

We keep the hiring process short on purpose. We respect that your time has value and we move at pace. No six-round marathons — if we think you're a fit, you'll know quickly.

01

Send your CV & a short note

Email us at support@black-dot.io with your CV and a paragraph on what you want to build here. No cover letter template needed — just be direct.

02

Introductory conversation

A 30-minute call with our leadership team. We'll talk about your background, what you care about, and what you're looking for. We'll also answer any questions you have — openly.

03

Technical or case discussion

A focused 60-minute discussion on a real-world scenario in your domain. No trick questions — we're testing how you think, not how well you've memorised textbooks.

04

Offer & onboarding

If there's a fit, we move fast. Offer, negotiation, and joining date — typically within a week of the final conversation. We'll have a structured onboarding plan ready on day one.

Always Open

Don't see a role that fits?

We're always interested in meeting exceptional people, regardless of whether we have a specific opening. Tell us what you do and what you want to build — we read everything.
